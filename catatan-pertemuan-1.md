# Meeting 1 – Introduction to Collaborative Computing



## Material Summary



## 1- Definition of Collaborative Computing

Collaborative computing uses hardware, software, and networks to help people work together toward a common goal. It involves people, technology, and agreed working processes.



## 2- Examples of Collaborative Computing

It is commonly used in everyday activities, such as Google Docs for shared documents, GitHub for software development, Zoom for online meetings, and Figma for collaborative design.



## 3- History of Digital Collaboration

Collaborative technology has developed through several milestones, starting with Douglas Engelbart's demonstration in 1968, the introduction of CSCW in 1984, the creation of Git in 2005, and the launch of GitHub in 2008.



## 4- The 3C Model

The 3C Model consists of three elements:



- **Communication**: Sharing information and ideas.

- **Coordination**: Organizing tasks and schedules.

- **Collaboration**: Creating documents, code, or designs together.



## 5- Benefits and Challenges

Collaborative computing makes teamwork faster, combines different skills, and allows work to be tracked. However, it also involves challenges such as communication problems, editing conflicts, data security, and differences in technology access
